Privacy

Last updated 26 September 2026. This is a plain-language template; have it reviewed before relying on it.

What we collect

Rigs is a static website. It has no accounts and does not run its own servers for the app. Your wallet address is read in your browser when you connect, and is public on the blockchain anyway.

Stored in your browser

Your theme, accent colour and last wallet connection method are kept in your browser's local storage. Clear site data to remove them.

Third parties

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and code from jsDelivr, reads Robinhood Chain through its public RPC, and, if enabled, connects wallets through Reown. Each has its own privacy policy.

On-chain data

Launches, trades and claims are public, permanent blockchain transactions. We cannot delete them.
