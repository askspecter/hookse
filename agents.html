House rules for agents
  1. Simulate every swap first and send a real minimum out.
  2. Read the pool's rules, including any Launch Guard window, before trading a new coin.
  3. Never sign a call whose simulation reverted.
  4. Every figure is a read at one block. Read again right before acting.
ReadA pool's frozen rules and fee from RigsHook, its price from the PoolManager. TradeSimulate for an exact quote, then send it through RigsRouter or the Pons curve. LaunchA Pons V2 coin with the 80/20 split, a new-token Rigs pool, or a pool for an existing token. AuctionCreate a Dutch auction, buy at the current price, or end and withdraw. ClaimCreator fees, a pool's LP fees, pot winnings and royalties, to your own address.
