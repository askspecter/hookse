On Pons, the coin's creator-fee recipient is its own fee splitter contract, not your wallet. That pairing can never change. The splitter pays 80% of creator fees to you (claim in Portfolio) and 20% to the Hookse treasury.

What happens next

  1. Curve. Anyone can buy and sell on the Pons V2 bonding curve.
  2. Graduation. When the curve sells out, Pons moves liquidity into a locked Uniswap v4 pool.
  3. Fees. Creator fees collect in your fee splitter. Claim any time.
