Buyback & burn

  1. 01Coins launch on Rigs

    Every coin launched through Rigs pays creator fees on its trades.

  2. 0220% goes to the treasury

    Each coin's own fee splitter sends 80% to its creator and 20% to the Rigs treasury.

  3. 0310% of that buys $RIGS

    10% of what the treasury earns is used to buy $RIGS on the market.

    It is sent to the dead address, where no one can ever move it. The total above is read live from the chain.
